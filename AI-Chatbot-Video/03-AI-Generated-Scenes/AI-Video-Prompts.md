# Part 4 — Individual AI Video Generation Prompts

Output order: **7. AI video generation prompts**. Copy one block at a time into your AI video tool.

**Settings for every clip:** 16:9, 1080p, 5–10 seconds, image-to-video with the character reference image (see `02-Character-References/`). Generate 2–3 takes and pick the most natural one.

**Phone and laptop screens:** the prompts ask for a plain green screen. In the editor, replace it with the real recording. If the green is not clean, cut to the full-screen recording instead.

**Save as:** `03-AI-Generated-Scenes/SC-0XX-AI.mp4` (add `-sokha`, `-dara` or `-broll` when a scene has two clips).
