# Part 2 — Reusable Character Reference Prompts

Output order: **3. Character reference prompts**.

## How to keep the faces the same (one simple method)

1. Generate **one reference image** for each character with the prompt below (front view, neutral background).
2. Pick the best image. Save it as `02-Character-References/Sokha-ref.png` and `Dara-ref.png`.
3. For every scene, use **image-to-video** (or "character reference" / "start frame") with that image, plus the scene prompt.
4. Never change the clothing words. Copy the full character block into every prompt.

**Why this works:** the AI tool copies the face, hair and clothes from the same picture each time, so it does not "invent" a new person.

## Shared settings

**Sokha's room (night):** Sokha's small, tidy bedroom in a Phnom Penh apartment at night: a light-wood desk, a desk lamp with warm light, a few books, a beige document folder, and a window with softly blurred city lights.

**Help-desk office (day):** A modern Cambodian university help-desk office in the morning: a light-wood counter and desk, white walls, a green potted plant, large windows with soft daylight, and one or two blurred colleagues far in the background.

**Style line (add to every AI prompt):** Photorealistic documentary style, 16:9, 24 fps, soft natural light, smooth slow camera movement, realistic modern Cambodian setting, calm and natural acting, realistic skin texture, mostly neutral colours with warm tones.

**Negative prompt (add to every AI prompt):** No readable text, no letters on screens or signs, no logos, no watermarks, no subtitles, no holograms, no neon, no glowing effects, no lens flares, no extra fingers, no change of face, hairstyle or clothing, no exaggerated emotion, no fast camera moves.
