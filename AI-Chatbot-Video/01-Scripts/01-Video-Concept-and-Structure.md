# Part 1 — Video Concept and Story Structure

Output order: **1. Concept** and **2. Six-chapter structure**. Scene IDs match every other file.

## 1. Video concept

**Title:** AI Chatbot Management System (Telegram Bot)

**One-line idea:** A student gets no answer at midnight, and a staff member answers the same questions all day. Our Telegram bot and dashboard fix both problems — and we show the real working system.

**Style:** Calm academic documentary. Short AI-generated story scenes (Sokha and Dara) + real screen recordings of Telegram and the dashboard + clean 2D motion graphics + the real presenter on camera.

**Audience:** University lecturers, students and project evaluators.

**Versions:** Version A (Khmer narration) and Version B (English narration). Same scenes, same order, same visuals. Only the voice-over, captions and a few language-specific graphics change.

**Recommended total duration:** about **6:46** (406 seconds, 49 scenes). With slower Khmer reading, allow up to about 7:00.

**Why this length:** it is long enough to show every feature in the original script one by one, but short enough for a class presentation slot.

**Format:** 1920×1080 (16:9), 25 fps, stereo audio. Export H.264 MP4.

**Typography (same in both versions):**
- Headings: Newsreader (English) · Kantumruy Pro or Noto Serif Khmer (Khmer)
- Captions and labels: Inter (English) · Kantumruy Pro (Khmer)
- Colours: background #FAF9F6, cards #FFFFFF, text #111827, secondary text #4B5563, borders #E5E7EB, one muted accent (for example #1F5F7A) used only for highlights.

**Honesty rules used in this plan:**
- Sokha and Dara are fictional. The end card says so.
- Every software scene uses the real system (branch `feature/all-screens`).
- The bot's knowledge grows only because staff add verified FAQs. The video never says the AI retrains itself.
- The results are from the team's own 57 test questions, not a public benchmark.
